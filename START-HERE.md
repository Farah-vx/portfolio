# Farah’s portfolio

## Open your portfolio
1. Extract the entire ZIP folder.
2. Open farah-portfolio/index.html in your browser.
Keep all files and folders together. No installation is needed.

## This version
1. Portfolio cover with your full-colour photo, name and tagline.
2. What I do: your studies and separate descriptions of front-end development, UI/UX and graphic design.
3. Animated curved project ribbon: drag, use arrows, filter, search and open projects.
4. Existing About Me and contact sections.

A consistent ivory, charcoal and taupe palette covers the whole site in light and dark modes. First-time visitors start in dark mode. Their chosen theme is saved in the browser. Pause motion stops automatic movement.

## Included projects
Luxe Japan; K Archive; Knowledge Graph: Driverless Cars & IoT; Harry Potter API; Creative Coding: Animated Forms; GemQuest; Sveltra Fitness App Prototype; Visual Design Explorations.

Images, local fonts, project videos and your PDF résumé are included. The vending machine project has been removed.

## Contact and résumé
Update the profile object at the top of script.js to change contact details or add your LinkedIn URL. Replace resume.pdf to update your résumé. Your latest uploaded résumé is included unchanged in resume.pdf.

## Upload
Upload the contents of farah-portfolio (index.html, style.css, ribbon.css, theme.css, script.js, images, resume.pdf and the entire assets folder) to your portfolio hosting project. Keep the folder structure intact. Use a separate portfolio repository to avoid overwriting Luxe Japan.
